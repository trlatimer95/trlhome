About Us

A Local Crew You Can Count On

TRL Home Solutions is a family-owned company based in Spokane Valley, Washington. We take on remodels, basements, bathrooms, decks, flooring and finish carpentry, and we still answer the call for the repairs and small jobs that keep a home running.

Our crew is led by the owners, and any subcontractors we bring in are vetted and directly supervised. We also work with local realtors to help sellers get homes market-ready and buyers get settled after closing.
