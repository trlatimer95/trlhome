What Is a Punchlist?

THE LIST BETWEEN "AS IS" AND "READY"

A punchlist is the list of repairs, fixes and finishing touches that stand between a house as it is today and the house it needs to be. It usually comes from a home inspection, a walkthrough with your realtor, or just your own notes.

Whether you're selling or buying, the list is rarely one big job. It's a dozen small ones, and that's exactly the kind of work we do.

For Sellers

Pre-Sale Punchlists

Getting a house ready to list, or responding to a buyer's inspection, means fixing the things that make buyers hesitate. We handle the list so you can focus on the move.

Need more than touch-ups? We also do full pre-sale remodels. See a recent one.

For Buyers

Post-Sale Punchlists

You just got the keys. We tackle the repair items from your inspection and the updates you want done before (or soon after) you move in.
